Ingredients

  • 1/2 ozGin
  • 1/2 ozMelon Liqueur
  • 1/2 ozRum
  • 1/2 ozTequila
  • 1/2 ozTriple Sec
  • 1/2 ozVodka
  • 1 ozSweet & Sour
  • 1 SplashClub Soda

Tokyo Tea

Stir

Instructions

  1. Add gin, melon liqueur, rum, tequila, triple sec, vodka and sweet-and-sour mix into a highball glass with ice and stir to combine.
  2. Top with the club soda.
  3. Optional: Garnish with a lemon wheel and maraschino cherry.
