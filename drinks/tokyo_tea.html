Ingredients

  • 2 cupsall-purpose flour
  • 2 tbspsugar
  • 2 tspbaking powder
  • 1/2 tspsalt
  • 1 3/4 cupsbuttermilk
  • 2large eggs
  • 4 tbspmelted butter, plus more for the pan

Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

Prep 10 min • Cook 15 min • Serves 4

Instructions

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a buttered skillet over medium heat. Pour in about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake.
  5. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook another 1 to 2 minutes until golden.
  6. Serve warm with butter and syrup.
