Ingredients

  • 1 ozBlue Curaçao
  • 1 1/5 ozSour Apple Pucker
  • 1 ozRaspberry Vodka
  • 1 ozGreen Apple Vodka
  • 1 ozSweet & Sour
  • 2 ozLemon Lime Soda
  • 2 ozWhite Cranberry Juice
  • 1 CandyJolly Rancher Candy

Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

Prep 10 min • Cook 15 min • Serves 4

Instructions

  1. Shake all ingredients until chilled.
  2. To make four equal size shots, add 1/2 oz. of the vodka.
  3. Get the pick wet and adhese it to the candy.
  4. Just hold the candy onto the wet pick until the connection hardens. This will take 10 to 20 seconds.
